CHAPTER 1: ABOUT NIMBUS SOFTECH

[1.1 Welcome Message]
Welcome to Nimbus Softech Private Limited. Whether you have joined us as a fresher straight out of college or as an experienced professional, we are glad you are here. This handbook explains how we work, what we expect from you, and what you can expect from us. It covers policies on leave, pay, benefits, travel, information security, conduct and much more. Please read it during your first week and keep it handy. When this handbook and an official policy document disagree, the policy document published on the HR portal (PeopleHub) takes priority, and HR will correct the handbook in its next version.

[1.2 Who We Are]
Nimbus Softech Private Limited is an Indian software services and product company headquartered in Mohali, Punjab. We build cloud-native applications, data platforms and AI solutions for customers in banking, retail, healthcare, logistics and education. The company was founded in 2011 by three engineers who had worked together on a payments platform. Today we have more than 2,400 employees across six offices in India, a small sales office in Dubai, and a fully remote team spread across twenty Indian states.

[1.3 Our History]
Nimbus Softech started in 2011 in a rented two-room office in Phase 8, Mohali, with eleven employees and one customer, a cooperative bank in Ludhiana. In 2014 we opened our Bengaluru office to be closer to product customers. In 2016 we launched our first product, NimbusPay, a payments reconciliation tool now used by more than ninety financial institutions. Offices in Pune and Hyderabad followed in 2018 and 2019. In 2021 we moved our headquarters to the current Nimbus Tower in IT City, Mohali. Gurugram and Chennai offices opened in 2023, and the Dubai sales office opened in 2025.

[1.4 Mission and Vision]
Our mission is to help organisations solve real business problems with reliable, well-engineered software. Our vision is to be the most trusted engineering partner for mid-sized enterprises in India and the Middle East by 2030. We measure ourselves by three things: whether our customers renew, whether our software runs without surprises in production, and whether our people would recommend Nimbus as a place to work to a friend.

[1.5 Our Core Values]
Nimbus Softech has five core values, known internally as CLEAR. Customer first means we understand the customer's problem before we write code. Learning always means we expect every employee to keep growing, and we fund that growth. Engineering excellence means we take pride in clean, tested, secure code. Accountability means we own our work, including our mistakes. Respect means we treat colleagues, customers and partners fairly, regardless of their role, background or location. These values are used in hiring, performance reviews and promotions.

[1.6 Leadership Team]
The company is led by the Chief Executive Officer, Raghav Malhotra, who is one of the three founders. The Chief Technology Officer is Meenakshi Rao, also a founder. The Chief Financial Officer is Sandeep Bansal. The Chief People Officer, who leads Human Resources, is Farah Siddiqui. The Chief Operating Officer is Arjun Nair. The Chief Information Security Officer is Kavita Deshpande. The leadership team holds an open Ask Me Anything session on the last Friday of every quarter, which all employees can join online.

[1.7 How the Company Is Organised]
Nimbus Softech is organised into business units and shared functions. The three business units are Financial Services, Retail and Logistics, and Healthcare and Education. Each business unit has its own delivery teams, solution architects and account managers. Shared functions such as Human Resources, Finance, Information Technology, Legal, Facilities, Information Security and Learning and Development support all business units. The Products division, which builds NimbusPay and NimbusInsight, reports directly to the Chief Technology Officer.

[1.8 Our Products]
Nimbus Softech owns two products. NimbusPay is a payments reconciliation platform that matches transactions across banks, payment gateways and internal ledgers. NimbusInsight is a data analytics platform that lets business users build dashboards without writing code. Both products are sold as software-as-a-service. Employees who want to try the products can request a sandbox account through the IT Service Portal at no cost to their project.

CHAPTER 3: WORKING HOURS AND ATTENDANCE

[3.1 Standard Working Week]
The standard working week at Nimbus Softech is five days, Monday to Friday, with a total of 45 hours including breaks. Saturdays and Sundays are weekly offs for most employees. Employees in customer support, infrastructure operations and some project teams may work on a rotational roster that includes weekends; they receive two weekly offs on other days instead. Your manager will tell you if you are on a roster when you join.

[3.2 Core Hours]
Our standard office timings are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Within this, core hours are 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM, when every employee is expected to be available for meetings and collaboration, whether they are in the office or working remotely. Outside core hours, employees may start between 8:00 AM and 11:00 AM and finish their day accordingly, as long as they complete nine hours including a one-hour lunch break and their team agrees on the arrangement.

[3.3 Shifts for Customer-Facing Teams]
Teams that support customers in other time zones may work in shifts. The UK shift runs from 1:00 PM to 10:00 PM IST. The US shift runs from 6:30 PM to 3:30 AM IST. Employees working the US shift receive a night shift allowance of Rs. 400 per night and company-arranged cab drop to their home. Employees working the UK shift receive a shift allowance of Rs. 200 per day. Shift allowances are paid with the following month's salary and are calculated from attendance records in PeopleHub.

[3.4 Recording Attendance]
Attendance is recorded automatically when you tap your ID card at the office entrance turnstiles. On remote working days, you must mark attendance by clicking Check In on the PeopleHub mobile app or website before 11:00 AM. If you forget to check in or your card fails, you can submit an attendance regularisation request in PeopleHub within seven days. More than three regularisation requests in a calendar month will be flagged to your manager.

[3.5 Late Arrival and Early Departure]
Occasional late arrival is understandable given traffic and weather. However, arriving after 11:00 AM without informing your manager more than three times in a month counts as half a day of casual leave for each additional instance. If you need to leave early for a personal reason, inform your manager and apply for a short leave in PeopleHub. Two short leaves of up to two hours each are allowed per month without any deduction.

[3.6 Overtime]
Nimbus Softech does not expect regular overtime. If project needs require you to work beyond 50 hours in a week, your manager must approve it in advance. Employees in grades L1 to L3 who work on a weekly off or a public holiday at the manager's request receive one compensatory off day for each full day worked. Compensatory off must be used within 60 days or it lapses. Employees in grades L4 and above do not receive compensatory off but may agree on flexible time off with their manager.

[3.7 Breaks]
Employees are entitled to a one-hour lunch break, normally taken between 12:30 PM and 2:30 PM, and two short tea breaks of fifteen minutes each. Employees working night shifts receive a forty-five-minute meal break and the company cafeteria remains open for them with a limited menu. Nursing mothers are entitled to two additional nursing breaks of thirty minutes each per day until their child turns fifteen months old.

[3.8 Working on Public Holidays]
If business needs require you to work on a public holiday, your manager must raise a holiday work request in PeopleHub at least two days in advance, except in emergencies. Employees in grades L1 to L5 receive double their daily basic pay for working on a national holiday, in addition to a compensatory off. Production support engineers on the on-call roster receive the on-call allowance instead, described in section 3.9.

[3.9 On-Call Support]
Engineers who support production systems may be placed on an on-call roster. On-call duty normally lasts one week, from Monday 10:00 AM to the following Monday 10:00 AM. The on-call allowance is Rs. 3,000 per week, plus Rs. 1,000 for every incident that requires more than one hour of work outside office hours. On-call engineers must respond to an alert within fifteen minutes and must stay within reach of their laptop and an internet connection. No employee should be on call for more than two weeks in any six-week period.

[3.10 Attendance for Interns]
Interns follow the same office timings as employees and must work from the office at least four days a week during their first two months. Interns record attendance the same way as employees. An intern's stipend is calculated on the days they are present, and unapproved absence of more than three days in a month may lead to the internship being ended early.

CHAPTER 4: LEAVE POLICY

[4.1 Overview of Leave Types]
Nimbus Softech offers several types of leave so that employees can rest, handle personal matters and care for their families. The main types are Paid Time Off (also called earned leave), casual leave, sick leave, maternity leave, paternity leave, adoption leave, bereavement leave, marriage leave, study leave, sabbatical and compensatory off. All leave is applied for and approved through PeopleHub. The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December.

[4.2 Paid Time Off]
Every full-time employee receives 24 days of Paid Time Off (PTO) per calendar year. PTO is credited at the rate of two days on the first day of each month. Employees who join in the middle of a year receive PTO for the remaining full months only. PTO is meant for vacations and planned personal time and should normally be applied for at least two weeks in advance for absences of three days or more. Managers should not refuse PTO without a genuine business reason, and must suggest alternative dates if they do.

[4.3 Carry Forward of Paid Time Off]
Unused Paid Time Off can be carried forward to the next calendar year, up to a maximum of 10 days. Any balance above 10 days on 31 December lapses, unless it is encashed as described in section 4.4. The carried-forward balance can be used at any time in the following year. Employees on long-term medical leave on 31 December may request HR to extend the carry-forward limit to 20 days for that year.

[4.4 Leave Encashment]
Employees in grades L1 to L6 may encash up to 8 days of unused Paid Time Off once a year, in December. Encashment is paid at the rate of the employee's daily basic salary and is taxable. To encash, submit an encashment request in PeopleHub between 1 and 15 December. At the time of leaving the company, all unused PTO up to a maximum of 30 days is encashed as part of the full and final settlement. Casual leave and sick leave cannot be encashed.

[4.5 Casual Leave]
Every employee receives 7 days of casual leave per calendar year, credited on 1 January. Casual leave is for short, unplanned personal needs such as a household emergency, a bank visit or a child's school function. Casual leave cannot be taken for more than three consecutive days and cannot be combined with Paid Time Off in the same continuous absence. Unused casual leave lapses on 31 December and is not carried forward.

[4.6 Sick Leave]
Every employee receives 10 days of sick leave per calendar year. Sick leave of up to two consecutive days does not require a medical certificate. For three or more consecutive days of sick leave, a medical certificate from a registered doctor must be uploaded in PeopleHub within three days of returning to work. Unused sick leave can be carried forward up to a maximum accumulated balance of 30 days, which acts as a safety net for longer illnesses.

[4.7 Extended Medical Leave]
If an employee has exhausted their sick leave and PTO because of a serious illness, injury or surgery, they may apply for extended medical leave of up to 60 days with full pay, once in their employment, after completing one year of service. The application must be supported by a hospital discharge summary or a specialist's certificate. Beyond this, unpaid medical leave of up to six months may be approved by the Chief People Officer.

[4.8 Maternity Leave]
Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave for their first two children, in line with the Maternity Benefit Act. For the third child onwards, maternity leave is 12 weeks. Up to 8 weeks of the leave can be taken before the expected delivery date. Employees who have a miscarriage or medical termination of pregnancy are entitled to 6 weeks of paid leave. After maternity leave, employees may request to work from home for up to three additional months, subject to the nature of their role.

[4.9 Paternity Leave]
Male employees and non-birthing partners are entitled to 15 working days of paid paternity leave, which must be taken within six months of the child's birth. Paternity leave can be taken in up to three blocks. Nimbus Softech encourages all eligible employees to take their full paternity leave, and managers should plan project work accordingly.

[4.10 Adoption Leave]
Employees who legally adopt a child below the age of three months are entitled to 12 weeks of paid adoption leave, starting from the date the child is handed over. Employees who adopt a child older than three months are entitled to 6 weeks of paid adoption leave. Non-primary caregivers in an adoption receive 15 working days of leave, the same as paternity leave. A copy of the adoption order must be uploaded in PeopleHub.

[4.11 Bereavement Leave]
In the unfortunate event of the death of an immediate family member, employees can take up to 5 days of paid bereavement leave. Immediate family includes spouse or partner, children, parents, parents-in-law, siblings and grandparents. Bereavement leave does not require prior approval; simply inform your manager by phone or message and apply in PeopleHub when you return. HR will reach out to offer support through the Employee Assistance Programme.

[4.12 Marriage Leave]
Employees can take 5 days of paid marriage leave for their own wedding, once during their employment with Nimbus Softech. Marriage leave must be taken within one month before or three months after the wedding date and can be combined with Paid Time Off. Please apply at least four weeks in advance so that your team can plan.

[4.13 Study Leave]
Employees with more than two years of service who are pursuing a degree or professional certification relevant to their role may take up to 10 days of paid study leave per year to prepare for and attend examinations. The course must be approved in advance by the Learning and Development team. Proof of examination registration must be uploaded with the leave request.

[4.14 Sabbatical]
Employees with at least five years of continuous service can apply for an unpaid sabbatical of between one and six months for higher studies, family care, social work or personal reasons. During a sabbatical, the employee's health insurance continues, but salary, leave accrual and bonus eligibility are paused. Sabbatical requests must be submitted at least three months in advance and approved by the business unit head and the Chief People Officer.

[4.15 Compensatory Off]
Compensatory off is granted to employees in grades L1 to L3 who work a full day on a weekly off or public holiday at their manager's request. Half a day of compensatory off is granted for four to eight hours of work. Compensatory off must be used within 60 days of being earned, after which it lapses automatically. It cannot be encashed.

[4.16 Loss of Pay]
If an employee is absent without any leave balance, the absence is recorded as Loss of Pay (LOP), and one day's gross salary is deducted for each day of LOP. Absence without informing the manager for three or more consecutive working days is treated as unauthorised absence and may lead to disciplinary action, as described in the Code of Conduct chapter.

[4.17 Applying for Leave]
To apply for leave, log in to PeopleHub, open the Leave tab, choose the leave type and dates, and add a short reason. Your reporting manager receives a notification and must approve or reject the request within three working days. If the manager does not act within three working days, the request is escalated to the manager's manager. You can see your balance for every leave type on the Leave tab at any time.

[4.18 Leave During Notice Period]
Employees serving their notice period may use Paid Time Off only with the approval of their manager and the HR business partner, because a smooth handover is required. Unused PTO at the time of exit is encashed as part of the full and final settlement, up to 30 days. Sick leave may be taken during the notice period as usual, with a medical certificate for absences of three or more days.

[4.19 Leave for Interns and Probationers]
Interns receive one day of paid leave for every month of internship, which cannot be carried forward. Employees on probation receive Paid Time Off, casual leave and sick leave like all other employees, but they may not use more than 6 days of Paid Time Off during their first six months.

[4.20 Other Leave Types at a Glance]
Besides the leave types described above, PeopleHub shows a few other leave types. Wellness Days are two paid days a year for rest, described in the Employee Wellbeing chapter. Volunteering Leave gives two paid days a year for NimbusCares activities. Restricted Holiday lets you take two optional festival holidays a year from the approved list. Short Leave allows two absences of up to two hours each per month. Gender transition leave of up to 30 paid days is described in the Diversity, Inclusion and Accessibility chapter.

[4.21 Leave and Public Holidays]
Public holidays and weekly offs that fall within a period of Paid Time Off are not counted as leave. For example, if you take PTO from Thursday to the following Tuesday, and Saturday and Sunday are your weekly offs, only four days of PTO are deducted. However, for maternity leave, sabbatical and extended medical leave, the leave is counted in calendar days, including weekends and holidays.

[4.22 Cancelling or Changing Leave]
You can cancel or change approved leave in PeopleHub before the leave starts, and your manager is notified automatically. If you return to work early from approved leave, update the leave request in PeopleHub so that the unused days are credited back to your balance. Leave that has already been taken cannot be converted to a different leave type after 30 days.

[4.23 Leave for Employees on Customer Deputation]
Employees deputed to customer sites follow the Nimbus leave policy, but must also inform the customer's project contact in advance where the customer requires it. If the customer's office is closed on a day that is a working day for Nimbus, the employee works remotely or takes leave as agreed with the project manager.

CHAPTER 6: COMPENSATION AND PAYROLL

[6.1 Salary Payment Date]
Salary for each month is credited to your registered bank account on the 1st of the following month. For example, salary for March is credited on 1 April. If the 1st falls on a Sunday or a bank holiday, salary is credited on the previous working day. Payroll for each month closes on the 20th, so changes such as new bank details, investment declarations or attendance corrections made after the 20th are reflected in the next month's salary.

[6.2 Salary Components]
Your monthly salary is made up of several components. Basic salary is 40 percent of your fixed annual compensation. House Rent Allowance (HRA) is 50 percent of basic salary for employees in metro cities and 40 percent for others. The remaining fixed amount is paid as a Special Allowance. In addition, the company contributes to your Provident Fund and gratuity, which are part of your cost to company (CTC) but are not paid monthly. Variable pay, where applicable, is paid annually as described in section 6.8.

[6.3 Understanding Your Payslip]
Payslips are published in PeopleHub on the 1st of every month under Payroll, then Payslips. The payslip shows earnings, deductions and net pay. Common deductions are employee Provident Fund contribution, professional tax, income tax deducted at source (TDS), and any recovery such as a salary advance or canteen dues. If you find an error in your payslip, raise a ticket under Payroll in PeopleHub within 30 days. Payslips for the last seven years remain available for download.

[6.4 Bank Account Changes]
Salary is paid only into a savings account held in the employee's own name. To change your salary account, upload a cancelled cheque or a bank statement showing your name, account number and IFSC code in PeopleHub before the 20th of the month. The change is verified by the payroll team and takes effect from the next salary credit. Nimbus Softech has a corporate salary arrangement with two banks, which offer zero-balance accounts; details are available on PeopleHub.

[6.5 Income Tax and Investment Declarations]
The company deducts income tax at source from your salary every month based on your chosen tax regime and declared investments. At the start of each financial year, in April, choose your tax regime and declare planned investments in PeopleHub. Between 1 and 31 January, you must upload proof of investments such as insurance receipts, rent receipts and ELSS statements. If proofs are not submitted by 31 January, tax will be recalculated without the declared investments, which may lead to higher deductions in February and March. Form 16 is issued by 15 June every year.

[6.6 Provident Fund]
All employees are members of the Employees' Provident Fund. The employee contributes 12 percent of basic salary each month and the company contributes an equal amount. You can view your Provident Fund balance on the EPFO member portal using your Universal Account Number (UAN), which is shown on your payslip. Employees can also make additional voluntary contributions through PeopleHub, up to 100 percent of basic salary.

[6.7 Gratuity]
Employees who complete five years of continuous service are eligible for gratuity when they leave the company, as per the Payment of Gratuity Act. Gratuity is calculated as 15 days of the last drawn basic salary for each completed year of service. Service of more than six months in the final year is rounded up to a full year. Gratuity is paid as part of the full and final settlement.

[6.8 Variable Pay and Annual Bonus]
Employees in grades L3 and above have a variable pay component, which is a percentage of fixed compensation that depends on company performance and individual performance. Variable pay is paid once a year in June, after annual results are finalised. Employees in grades L1 and L2 receive an annual performance bonus instead, of up to one month's basic salary. To receive variable pay or a bonus, an employee must be on the company's rolls on the payout date and must have joined before 1 January of the previous year's cycle.

[6.9 Annual Salary Revision]
Salaries are reviewed once a year, with revised salaries effective from 1 April. The revision depends on your performance rating, your position within the salary band for your grade, and the company's overall budget for the year. Revision letters are published in PeopleHub by 30 April. Employees who joined after 1 October of the previous year are reviewed in the following cycle, not the current one.

[6.10 Salary Advance]
In a genuine emergency such as a medical situation or a family event, employees who have completed six months of service may request a salary advance of up to two months' net salary. The advance is interest-free and is recovered in equal monthly instalments over a maximum of six months. Only one advance can be outstanding at a time. Requests are made in PeopleHub and approved by the HR business partner and the Finance team.

[6.11 Referral Bonus]
Employees who refer candidates who are hired and complete three months with the company receive a referral bonus. The bonus is Rs. 25,000 for referrals hired into grades L1 to L3, Rs. 50,000 for grades L4 and L5, and Rs. 75,000 for grades L6 and above. Referrals for hard-to-fill roles marked as Hot Jobs on the careers portal earn an extra Rs. 15,000. Managers do not receive referral bonuses for candidates who join their own teams. Referrals are submitted through the Refer a Friend section of PeopleHub.

[6.12 Salary Confidentiality]
Your compensation is confidential between you and the company. You are free to discuss your own pay with others if you choose, but employees who handle payroll or HR data must never disclose another employee's salary. Questions about your compensation should be directed to your manager or HR business partner, not to colleagues in the payroll team directly.

CHAPTER 7: BENEFITS AND INSURANCE

[7.1 Group Health Insurance]
All employees are covered under the company's group health insurance policy from their date of joining. The policy covers hospitalisation expenses for the employee, spouse or partner, up to two children and either parents or parents-in-law. The base sum insured is Rs. 5,00,000 per family per year on a floater basis. Pre-existing diseases are covered from day one, and there is no waiting period for maternity benefits. The insurance provider is administered through a third-party administrator, and the e-card can be downloaded from the Benefits section of PeopleHub.

[7.2 Maternity Cover Under Health Insurance]
The group health insurance policy covers maternity hospitalisation expenses up to Rs. 75,000 for a normal delivery and Rs. 1,00,000 for a caesarean delivery. Newborn babies are covered from the first day of birth without separate enrolment, but the baby must be added to the policy in PeopleHub within 30 days of birth for continued cover.

[7.3 Top-Up Insurance]
Employees can buy additional top-up health insurance through the company at group rates. Top-up options are Rs. 5,00,000, Rs. 10,00,000 and Rs. 20,00,000, which apply after the base cover of Rs. 5,00,000 is used up. The premium for top-up cover is deducted from salary in twelve equal instalments. Top-up cover can be chosen during the annual enrolment window in March, or within 30 days of joining.

[7.4 Parental Insurance Choice]
Employees can cover either their own parents or their parents-in-law under the group health policy, but not both. The choice is made at the time of joining and can be changed only during the annual enrolment window in March. Parents above 80 years of age are covered with a co-payment of 20 percent of the claim amount.

[7.5 Making a Health Insurance Claim]
For planned hospitalisation at a network hospital, request cashless pre-authorisation through the third-party administrator's app at least 48 hours before admission. For emergencies, inform the administrator within 24 hours of admission. For treatment at a non-network hospital, pay the bills and submit a reimbursement claim with original bills and the discharge summary within 30 days of discharge. The HR Benefits team can help with any difficulty at benefits@nimbussoftech.example.

[7.6 Group Term Life Insurance]
All employees are covered by a group term life insurance policy with a sum assured of three times their annual fixed compensation, subject to a minimum of Rs. 20,00,000. The premium is paid fully by the company. Employees should update their nominee details in PeopleHub so that claims can be settled without delay.

[7.7 Group Personal Accident Insurance]
Employees are covered by a group personal accident policy that pays compensation in the event of accidental death or disability, whether at work or outside work, anywhere in the world. The sum assured is five times the annual fixed compensation, subject to a maximum of Rs. 1,00,00,000. Temporary total disability due to an accident is compensated at 1 percent of the sum assured per week, for up to 52 weeks.

[7.8 Annual Health Check-Up]
Employees aged 30 and above are entitled to a free annual health check-up at partner diagnostic centres. Employees below 30 can avail the check-up once every two years. Spouses of employees aged 35 and above can also avail one free check-up per year. Appointments are booked through the Wellness section of PeopleHub.

[7.9 Employee Assistance Programme]
The Employee Assistance Programme (EAP) provides free, confidential counselling for employees and their immediate family members. Counsellors can help with stress, relationships, parenting, grief, financial worries and work challenges. Up to eight counselling sessions per person per year are free. The EAP helpline is available 24 hours a day, seven days a week, in English, Hindi, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and Marathi. Nimbus Softech never receives information about who used the service.

[7.10 Meal Benefits]
Employees working from any Nimbus office receive a subsidised lunch in the office cafeteria at Rs. 40 per meal. Breakfast is free for employees who reach the office before 9:30 AM. Employees may also opt for meal cards loaded with up to Rs. 2,200 per month as part of their salary structure, which is tax-efficient under current rules. Tea, coffee and fruit are available free of charge throughout the day.

[7.11 Fitness Benefit]
Employees can claim up to Rs. 12,000 per year as a fitness reimbursement for gym memberships, yoga classes, swimming, sports coaching or fitness equipment. Claims are submitted through the Expenses section of PeopleHub with invoices. Offices in Mohali, Bengaluru and Hyderabad also have on-site gyms, which are free for employees and open from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM.

[7.12 Childcare Support]
Employees with children below six years of age can claim a childcare allowance of Rs. 5,000 per month per child, for up to two children, for crèche or daycare fees. The Mohali headquarters has an on-site crèche, which is free for employees' children aged six months to five years. Places at the crèche are limited and allocated on a first-come basis through PeopleHub.

[7.13 National Pension System]
Employees can choose to have the company contribute up to 10 percent of their basic salary to the National Pension System (NPS) as part of their salary structure. This reduces taxable income under applicable sections of the Income Tax Act. The choice is made during the investment declaration window in April and can be changed once a year.

[7.14 Mobile and Internet Reimbursement]
Employees in grades L3 and above, and employees on the on-call roster, can claim reimbursement for mobile and home internet bills of up to Rs. 1,500 per month. Employees in grades L1 and L2 can claim up to Rs. 800 per month for home internet on remote working days. Claims are submitted monthly in the Expenses section of PeopleHub with copies of bills.

Nimbus Softech has partnerships with several companies that offer discounts to employees, including electronics stores, airlines, hotels and online learning platforms. The current list of discounts and the codes to use are published every month on the Perks page of the company intranet, NimbusNet.

Employees who complete 5, 10 and 15 years of service receive long service awards. At five years, employees receive a gift voucher of Rs. 25,000 and a certificate. At ten years, employees receive a gift voucher of Rs. 75,000 and three additional days of Paid Time Off in that year. CHAPTER 9: INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY AND SECURITY

[9.1 Your Company Laptop]
Every employee receives a company laptop on their first day. Engineers receive a laptop with at least 32 GB of memory, and other roles receive a laptop with at least 16 GB. Employees can choose between a Windows laptop and a MacBook, subject to project requirements. The laptop remains company property and must be returned on your last working day. Laptops are replaced every 3 years, or earlier if they develop a hardware fault that cannot be repaired.

[9.2 Laptop Care and Loss]
Take care of your laptop as you would your own. Never leave it unattended in a car, a café or an airport. If your laptop is lost or stolen, report it to the IT helpdesk and the security team within two hours, and file a police report within 24 hours. IT will remotely lock and wipe the device. The first loss is not charged to the employee if a police report is filed. A second loss within three years may be charged at the depreciated value of the laptop.

[9.3 Accounts and Single Sign-On]
On joining, you receive a Nimbus email account and a single sign-on (SSO) account that gives you access to PeopleHub, the IT Service Portal, NimbusNet and other internal systems. Your username is your first name followed by a dot and your last name. Access to project-specific systems such as source code repositories and cloud accounts is requested by your manager through the IT Service Portal.

[9.4 Password Rules]
Your SSO password must be at least 14 characters long and must not contain your name or username. We recommend using a passphrase of four or more random words. Passwords expire every 180 days. Never share your password with anyone, including your manager or the IT helpdesk; IT staff will never ask for it. Use the company-approved password manager, NimbusVault, to store passwords for other systems.

[9.5 Multi-Factor Authentication]
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all company systems. You must install the approved authenticator app on your phone during onboarding. If you change or lose your phone, contact the IT helpdesk to reset MFA; you will need to verify your identity through a video call with your ID card. SMS-based one-time passwords are not allowed for company systems because they can be intercepted.

[9.6 Office Wi-Fi]
Each Nimbus office has three Wi-Fi networks. NimbusSecure is for company laptops and connects automatically using your SSO login; you do not need a separate password. NimbusMobile is for personal phones and tablets that have the company's mobile management app installed. NimbusGuest is for visitors; guest Wi-Fi passwords are issued by the reception desk and are valid for one day. Never connect a company laptop to NimbusGuest.

[9.7 VPN and Remote Access]
When working from outside the office, connect to the company VPN, NimbusConnect, before accessing internal systems. The VPN client is pre-installed on company laptops. Customer systems may require a separate VPN provided by the customer; follow the customer's rules for that VPN. Do not use public Wi-Fi in cafés, hotels or airports without the VPN, and prefer your phone's hotspot when possible.

[9.8 Approved Software]
Only software from the Nimbus Software Centre may be installed on company laptops. The Software Centre includes common development tools, browsers, office applications and communication tools. If you need software that is not listed, raise a software request in the IT Service Portal; the security team reviews it within three working days. Pirated or cracked software is strictly prohibited and is grounds for disciplinary action.

[9.9 Generative AI Tools]
Employees may use company-approved generative AI tools for work, including the enterprise AI assistant available through NimbusNet. Do not paste customer data, source code belonging to customers, personal data of employees, or confidential company information into public AI tools that are not approved by the security team. Code produced with the help of AI tools must be reviewed and tested like any other code, and the employee who commits it remains responsible for it.

[9.10 Data Classification]
Company information is classified into four levels. Public information can be shared with anyone, such as marketing material. Internal information is for employees only, such as this handbook. Confidential information is limited to people who need it, such as customer contracts and salary data. Restricted information is the most sensitive, such as customer production data, encryption keys and passwords, and may only be accessed by named individuals with written approval.

[9.11 Handling Customer Data]
Customer data must stay in the customer's environment or in Nimbus systems approved for that customer. Never copy customer production data to your laptop, personal email, USB drives or personal cloud storage. If you need realistic data for testing, use masked or synthetic data. Any exception requires written approval from the customer and the Chief Information Security Officer.

[9.12 USB Drives and Removable Media]
USB storage devices are blocked on company laptops by default. If your work requires removable media, such as for a hardware project, request an exception through the IT Service Portal with your manager's approval. Approved exceptions are valid for a maximum of 90 days and only for encrypted company-issued drives.

[9.13 Email and Phishing]
Be careful with emails that ask you to click a link, open an attachment, share credentials or make a payment urgently, even if they appear to come from a senior leader. Use the Report Phishing button in your email client to report suspicious emails; this sends the email to the security team. The security team runs simulated phishing tests every quarter. Employees who click on three simulated phishing emails in a year must complete an additional security awareness module.

[9.14 Reporting Security Incidents]
A security incident is anything that may put company or customer information at risk, such as a lost laptop, a suspicious email you clicked on, malware warnings, or data sent to the wrong person. Report every incident immediately to security@nimbussoftech.example or call extension 7777. Reporting quickly is always better than trying to fix things yourself. Employees are never punished for reporting an honest mistake promptly.

[9.15 Personal Devices]
You may use your personal phone for company email and chat only after installing the company's mobile device management app, which separates work data from personal data. The company can wipe only the work data on your phone, never your personal photos or apps. Personal laptops must not be used to access company or customer systems under any circumstances.

[9.16 Clean Desk and Screen Lock]
Lock your laptop screen whenever you leave your desk, even for a minute, using Windows+L on Windows or Control+Command+Q on a Mac. Laptops lock automatically after five minutes of inactivity. At the end of the day, do not leave confidential documents on your desk, and put printed confidential material in the locked shredding bins on each floor.

[9.17 Source Code and Repositories]
All source code is stored in the company's approved source code platform. Never upload company or customer code to personal repositories or public code-sharing sites. Commits must be made with your Nimbus email address. Secrets such as API keys and passwords must never be committed to code; use the approved secrets manager instead. Automated scanners check every commit and block those that contain secrets.

[9.18 Monitoring and Privacy]
To protect company and customer data, Nimbus Softech monitors company devices, networks and accounts for security threats. This includes scanning for malware, blocking dangerous websites and detecting data leaks. Monitoring is carried out by the security team under strict rules and is not used to track individual productivity. Personal use of company devices should be minimal, and employees should have no expectation of privacy on company systems.

[9.19 Acceptable Use of the Internet]

CHAPTER 11: CODE OF CONDUCT AND ETHICS

[11.1 Purpose of the Code of Conduct]
The Code of Conduct sets out the standards of behaviour that every Nimbus Softech employee must follow. It protects our people, our customers and our reputation. Every employee must read the Code and accept it in PeopleHub within the first week of joining and again every year in April. Breaking the Code can lead to disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment.

[11.2 Respect at Work]
Every employee has the right to work in an environment free from discrimination, bullying and harassment. Nimbus Softech does not tolerate discrimination based on gender, religion, caste, region, language, age, disability, sexual orientation, marital status or any other personal characteristic. Disagreements about work should be expressed respectfully, focusing on ideas rather than people. Shouting, insults, threats and public humiliation are never acceptable, regardless of seniority.

[11.3 Prevention of Sexual Harassment]
Nimbus Softech has a zero-tolerance policy on sexual harassment, in line with the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act. Each office has an Internal Committee (IC), headed by a senior woman employee and including an external member from a non-governmental organisation. Complaints can be made in writing to the IC at ic@nimbussoftech.example within three months of the incident. All complaints are handled confidentially, and retaliation against a complainant or witness is itself a serious violation.

[11.4 Conflict of Interest]
A conflict of interest happens when your personal interests could affect your decisions at work. Examples include hiring a relative into your team, owning shares in a vendor you are selecting, or working part-time for a competitor or customer. You must declare any actual or potential conflict of interest in the Declarations section of PeopleHub as soon as you become aware of it. Declaring a conflict is not wrong; hiding it is.

[11.5 Outside Employment and Freelancing]
Full-time employees may not take up any other paid employment while working for Nimbus Softech. Occasional unpaid activities such as open-source contributions, teaching at a college, writing blogs or speaking at conferences are encouraged, as long as they do not use confidential information, do not compete with the company and do not affect your work. Paid freelancing, consulting or running a side business requires written approval from the Chief People Officer.

[11.6 Gifts and Hospitality]
Employees may accept gifts from customers, vendors or partners only if the gift is of modest value, not more than Rs. 2,000, and is not cash or a cash equivalent such as a gift card. Gifts above this value must be politely declined or handed over to HR, which donates them to charity. Meals and events offered by business partners may be accepted if they are reasonable and have a clear business purpose. Gifts must never be accepted during an ongoing vendor selection or contract negotiation.

[11.7 Anti-Bribery and Corruption]
Nimbus Softech follows a strict anti-bribery policy. No employee may offer, give, request or accept a bribe, kickback or any improper payment, directly or through someone else, to win business or gain an advantage. This applies to dealings with government officials and private companies alike. Facilitation payments, which are small payments to speed up routine government actions, are also not allowed. Report any request for a bribe to the Legal team immediately.

[11.8 Confidentiality]
Employees must keep confidential information about the company, customers and colleagues secure, both during and after their employment. This includes business plans, pricing, customer lists, source code, product designs and personal data. Confidentiality obligations are part of the employment agreement and continue for three years after leaving the company, and indefinitely for trade secrets and personal data.

[11.9 Social Media]
You are free to use social media in your personal capacity, but remember that your posts can affect the company's reputation. Do not share confidential information, customer names, project details or photos of office screens and whiteboards. Make clear that your views are your own. Only the Corporate Communications team may speak on behalf of Nimbus Softech on social media or to journalists. If a journalist contacts you, refer them to press@nimbussoftech.example.

[11.10 Use of Company Resources]
Company resources such as laptops, internet, cloud accounts, printers and office supplies are provided for business use. Limited personal use, such as checking personal email during a break, is acceptable. Using company resources for illegal activities, cryptocurrency mining, running personal businesses, or accessing offensive content is prohibited. Cloud resources created for training or experiments must be deleted when no longer needed to avoid unnecessary costs.

[11.11 Alcohol, Drugs and Smoking]
Employees must not report to work under the influence of alcohol or illegal drugs. Alcohol may be served only at company-approved events, and employees must drink responsibly. Smoking and vaping are allowed only in designated smoking zones outside office buildings. Possession or use of illegal drugs on company premises leads to immediate termination.

[11.12 Whistleblower Policy]
If you become aware of fraud, bribery, data misuse, safety risks or any serious violation of the Code, you are encouraged to report it. Reports can be made to your manager, HR, the Legal team, or anonymously through the Ethics Helpline at ethics@nimbussoftech.example, which is managed by an independent firm. All reports are investigated by the Ethics Committee. Nimbus Softech strictly prohibits retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith.

[11.13 Disciplinary Process]
When a possible breach of the Code is reported, HR conducts a fair investigation, which includes hearing the employee's side. Depending on the seriousness of the breach, outcomes can include a verbal warning, a written warning, a final written warning, withholding of increment or bonus, demotion, or termination of employment. Serious misconduct such as fraud, violence, sexual harassment or theft may lead to immediate termination without notice.

[11.14 Grievance Redressal]
Employees who have a complaint about their work, their manager, a policy or the work environment should first try to resolve it with their manager. If that is not possible, the employee may raise a formal grievance with their HR business partner through PeopleHub. HR will acknowledge the grievance within two working days and aim to resolve it within 15 working days. If the employee is not satisfied, they may appeal to the Chief People Officer, whose decision is final.

[11.15 Political and Religious Neutrality]
Nimbus Softech respects every employee's personal beliefs. However, employees must not campaign for political parties, collect donations for political causes, or promote religious views at the workplace or on company communication channels. Festivals of all communities are celebrated in our offices in an inclusive way, and participation is always voluntary.

CHAPTER 12: PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT AND CAREER GROWTH

[12.1 Performance Cycle]
Nimbus Softech follows an annual performance cycle from 1 April to 31 March. At the start of the cycle, in April, every employee sets four to six goals with their manager in PeopleHub. A mid-year check-in happens in October, where goals can be updated if project priorities have changed. The annual review takes place in March, and final ratings are shared in the first week of April. Salary revisions based on these ratings take effect from 1 April.

[12.2 Setting Goals]
Goals should be specific, measurable and linked to the needs of your project, team or business unit. At least one goal must be a learning goal, such as completing a certification or learning a new technology. Engineers should include at least one goal on code quality or reliability. Goals are agreed between the employee and manager, not imposed, and both must sign them off in PeopleHub by 30 April.

[12.3 Continuous Feedback]
Managers should hold a one-to-one conversation with each team member at least once every two weeks. These conversations are for discussing progress, blockers, feedback and career aspirations, not only project status. Employees can also request feedback from colleagues at any time using the Feedback feature in PeopleHub. Feedback given through PeopleHub is visible to the employee and their manager.

[12.4 Self-Assessment and 360 Feedback]
In March, every employee writes a self-assessment against their goals and the CLEAR values. Employees also nominate three to five colleagues, including at least one from outside their immediate team, to give 360-degree feedback. Managers review the self-assessment and feedback before writing their own assessment.

[12.5 Performance Ratings]
Nimbus Softech uses a five-point rating scale. A rating of 5 means Exceptional, for performance far above expectations. A rating of 4 means Exceeds Expectations. A rating of 3 means Meets Expectations, which is a good rating and is expected for most employees. A rating of 2 means Partially Meets Expectations. A rating of 1 means Does Not Meet Expectations. Ratings are reviewed in calibration meetings to make sure they are fair across teams.

[12.6 Calibration]
Calibration meetings bring together managers within a business unit to review proposed ratings together. The aim is to make sure that a rating of 4 in one team means the same as a rating of 4 in another team, and to reduce individual bias. HR business partners facilitate calibration. Managers may change ratings after calibration and must explain the reason to the employee.

[12.7 Grades and Career Levels]
Nimbus Softech has eight career grades, from L1 to L8. Grades L1 to L3 are early-career roles, L4 and L5 are senior individual contributor and first-level manager roles, L6 and L7 are principal and senior leadership roles, and L8 is reserved for the executive leadership team. Each grade has a defined salary band, expected responsibilities and typical job titles. The detailed grade descriptions appear in the table later in this chapter.

[12.8 Promotions]
Promotions are considered once a year, in the annual cycle, with an effective date of 1 April. To be considered, an employee must have spent at least 18 months in their current grade and must have received a rating of 4 or 5 in the most recent cycle, or a rating of 3 in the last two cycles along with demonstrated performance at the next level. Promotion nominations come from managers and are reviewed by a promotion panel that includes leaders from outside the employee's team.

[12.9 Technical and Management Tracks]
From grade L4 onwards, employees can grow in either the technical track or the management track. The technical track includes roles such as Senior Engineer, Lead Engineer, Principal Engineer and Distinguished Engineer. The management track includes roles such as Engineering Manager, Delivery Manager, Director and Vice President. Both tracks have equal pay bands at the same grade, so employees do not need to become managers to grow.

[12.10 Internal Job Postings]
Open roles are posted on the Internal Job Posting (IJP) board in PeopleHub before they are advertised externally. Employees who have completed 12 months in their current role and have a rating of 3 or above can apply. You must inform your current manager before applying, but your manager cannot block an application. If selected, the move happens within 60 days, after a handover plan is agreed.

[12.11 Performance Improvement Plan]
An employee who receives a rating of 1, or a rating of 2 in two consecutive cycles, is placed on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP). A PIP lasts 60 to 90 days and sets out clear, achievable goals, the support the company will provide, and how progress will be measured. The employee meets their manager and HR every two weeks during the PIP. If the goals are met, the PIP is closed successfully. If not, the company may extend the PIP once or end employment with notice.

[12.12 Mentoring]
Every new employee is assigned a buddy for the first three months, who helps with practical questions about the office, tools and team. In addition, employees in grades L1 to L4 can request a mentor from a more senior grade outside their reporting line through the Mentoring section of PeopleHub. Mentoring relationships normally last six months and meet at least once a month.

[12.13 Recognition Awards]
Employees can recognise each other through the Kudos feature on NimbusNet, which comes with reward points that can be redeemed for vouchers. Every quarter, each business unit presents Spot Awards worth Rs. 10,000 to outstanding contributors. Every year, the company presents the CLEAR Awards for employees who best demonstrate each of our five values, with a prize of Rs. 1,00,000 and a trophy for each winner.

Learning always is one of the CLEAR values, and Nimbus Softech invests in every employee's growth. Learning hours are tracked in NimbusLearn, the company's learning platform, and are part of the annual goals discussed in chapter 12.

[13.2 NimbusLearn Platform]
NimbusLearn is the company's learning platform, available through single sign-on. It hosts internal courses created by Nimbus experts, licensed content from online learning providers, recorded tech talks, and the mandatory compliance courses. Courses completed on NimbusLearn are automatically added to your learning record. The full internal course catalogue is listed at the end of this chapter.

[13.3 Mandatory Courses]
All employees must complete four mandatory courses on NimbusLearn within their first 30 days and then every year by 30 June. These are Information Security Awareness, Prevention of Sexual Harassment, Code of Conduct and Anti-Bribery, and Data Privacy Basics. Employees who do not complete mandatory courses on time lose access to non-essential systems until they do, and their annual rating may be affected.

[13.4 Certification Sponsorship]
Nimbus Softech pays for professional certifications that are relevant to your role, such as cloud certifications from major cloud providers, Kubernetes certifications, project management certifications and security certifications. The company pays the examination fee for the first attempt, and for one re-attempt if the first is unsuccessful. Employees who pass receive a certification bonus of Rs. 10,000 for associate-level certifications and Rs. 25,000 for professional or expert-level certifications.

[13.5 Certification Approval Process]
To get a certification sponsored, apply in the Learning section of PeopleHub before booking the exam. The application needs your manager's approval and must show how the certification relates to your current or planned role. Once approved, the Learning and Development team books the exam voucher for you. Do not pay for the exam yourself and claim it later, because self-booked exams are not reimbursed.

[13.6 Learning Budget]
Every employee has an annual learning budget for external courses, books and conference passes. The budget is Rs. 30,000 per year for grades L1 to L3, Rs. 50,000 for grades L4 and L5, and Rs. 75,000 for grades L6 and above. Unused learning budget does not carry forward to the next year. Expenses are claimed in the Expenses section of PeopleHub under the Learning category, with your manager's approval.

[13.7 Conferences]
Employees may attend up to one external technical conference in India per year, using their learning budget for the pass and the travel policy for travel and stay. Employees who are selected to speak at a recognised conference receive full sponsorship of the pass, travel and stay, outside their learning budget, along with a speaker bonus of Rs. 15,000. Talks must be reviewed by the Corporate Communications team before submission.

[13.8 Higher Education Support]
Employees with at least three years of service who are pursuing a part-time or executive degree relevant to their role, such as an MTech, MBA or MS, may receive tuition support of up to 50 percent of fees, capped at Rs. 3,00,000 over the duration of the programme. Employees who receive higher education support must stay with the company for two years after the support ends, or repay a proportionate amount.

[13.9 Training Bond]
Employees who receive certification sponsorship or external training worth more than Rs. 1,00,000 in a single programme sign a training agreement. If the employee leaves within 12 months of completing the programme, they repay the cost on a proportionate basis. Mandatory courses, internal courses and standard certifications costing less than Rs. 1,00,000 do not have any bond.

[13.10 Tech Talks and Communities]
Every Thursday from 4:00 PM to 5:00 PM, Nimbus hosts an internal Tech Talk, where an employee presents a technology, a project learning or a post-incident review. Talks are streamed online and recorded on NimbusLearn. Employees can also join communities of practice for Java, Python, frontend, cloud, data, DevOps, testing, security and AI, which meet every month and maintain shared guidelines.

[13.11 Hackathons]
Nimbus Softech holds a company-wide hackathon called NimbusHack twice a year, in July and January. Teams of two to five employees have 36 hours to build a working prototype on a theme announced in advance. Winning teams receive cash prizes of Rs. 1,50,000 for first place, Rs. 1,00,000 for second and Rs. 50,000 for third, and the best ideas may be funded as internal projects.

CHAPTER 14: HYBRID AND REMOTE WORK

[14.1 Hybrid Work Model]
Nimbus Softech follows a hybrid work model for most roles. Employees attached to an office are expected to work from the office at least 3 days a week, and can work from home on the other days. Teams agree on common office days, called anchor days, so that team members meet in person. Most teams choose Tuesday and Wednesday as anchor days. Your manager will tell you your team's anchor days.

[14.2 Fully Remote Roles]
Some roles are designated as fully remote at the time of hiring. Fully remote employees are not attached to an office and work from home, but must live in India and must travel to an office for team events at least twice a year, at company cost. An existing office-attached employee may request to become fully remote for personal reasons such as family care, which requires approval from their business unit head and HR.

[14.3 Work From Home Setup Allowance]
Every employee receives a one-time work from home setup allowance of Rs. 15,000 after completing their probation, to buy a chair, desk, monitor, headphones or other equipment for a comfortable home workspace. Fully remote employees receive Rs. 30,000 instead. Claims must be supported by invoices and submitted within six months of becoming eligible.

[14.4 Expectations When Working Remotely]
When working from home, employees must be reachable during core hours, 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM, through the company chat and video calling tools. Keep your chat status updated, keep your camera on in team meetings where possible, and respond to messages within a reasonable time. Work from a quiet place where confidential conversations cannot be overheard, and never let family members use your company laptop.

[14.5 Working From Another City]
Employees may work from a city other than their base location for up to 30 days a year, for example while visiting family, provided they inform their manager and have a reliable internet connection. Working from outside India is not allowed, even temporarily, because of tax, legal and data protection rules, except on approved business travel.

[14.6 Internet Requirements]
Employees working from home must have a broadband connection with at least 50 Mbps speed, and a mobile hotspot as a backup. Internet reimbursement is available as described in the Benefits chapter. If your internet fails during working hours and cannot be fixed quickly, inform your manager and move to the nearest Nimbus office or a safe location with good connectivity.

[14.7 Hot Desks and Desk Booking]
Most Nimbus offices use hot desks, which means employees do not have permanently assigned desks. Book a desk up to two weeks in advance using the Desk Booking feature on the NimbusNet app. Desks not checked in by 11:00 AM are released for others. Lockers are available for employees to store personal items, and can be booked monthly through the same app.

[14.8 Remote Onboarding]
Employees hired into fully remote roles receive their laptop by courier at least two working days before their joining date, along with a welcome kit. On the first day, they join a video call with HR at 9:30 AM to complete documents and identity checks, and then attend the three-day virtual induction. Within their first three months, remote employees are invited to spend one week at their team's nearest office, at company cost, to meet colleagues in person.

[14.9 Staying Connected as a Remote Employee]
Remote employees can feel isolated if they are not included. Teams with remote members must hold all team meetings on MeetRoom, even when most people are in the office, so that everyone can participate equally. Decisions made in corridor conversations should be written in the team channel. The Remote Employees Network on NimbusChat organises virtual coffee chats every Wednesday and an annual meet-up in one of the offices.

[14.10 Coworking Spaces]
Fully remote employees who find it difficult to work from home can use a coworking space near their home for up to 10 days a month, at company cost, through the coworking partner listed on NimbusNet. Coworking spaces must offer a private, secure network or the employee must use the VPN, and confidential calls must be taken in a closed room.

[14.11 Equipment Support for Remote Employees]
If a remote employee's laptop fails, IT ships a replacement within two working days, or the employee can collect one from the nearest office the same day. Faulty laptops are collected from the employee's home by courier at no cost. Remote employees should keep the laptop's original box and charger for returns.

[14.12 Security When Working Remotely]
Never leave your laptop unlocked when family members or visitors are nearby. Do not print confidential documents at home. Do not discuss customer matters in public places where you can be overheard. Change the default password on your home Wi-Fi router and keep its software up to date. Remote employees must complete the same security courses as office-based employees.

CHAPTER 15: HEALTH, SAFETY AND EMERGENCIES

[15.1 Safety Commitment]
Nimbus Softech is committed to providing a safe and healthy workplace for employees, visitors and contractors. Each office has a Safety Officer appointed by the Facilities team and a team of trained volunteers called Floor Wardens. Every employee is responsible for following safety rules and reporting hazards, such as loose wires, wet floors or blocked fire exits, through the Facilities section of the IT Service Portal.

[15.2 Fire Safety]
All offices are equipped with smoke detectors, sprinklers, fire extinguishers and clearly marked emergency exits. When the fire alarm sounds, stop work immediately, leave your belongings, and walk calmly to the nearest emergency exit. Do not use lifts during a fire. Follow the instructions of the Floor Wardens and gather at the assembly point shown on the evacuation map near each lift lobby. Do not return to the building until the Safety Officer gives the all-clear.

[15.3 Evacuation Drills]
Each office conducts a fire evacuation drill twice a year, normally in June and December. Drills are announced in advance by email but the exact time is not shared. Participation is compulsory for everyone present in the building, including visitors. The target time for full evacuation of any Nimbus office is five minutes.

[15.4 First Aid]
First aid kits are available at the reception and on every floor, near the pantry. Each floor has at least two employees trained in first aid and cardiopulmonary resuscitation (CPR), whose names and photos are displayed near the first aid kit. The Mohali, Bengaluru and Hyderabad offices have a medical room with a nurse available from 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM on working days. Automated external defibrillators (AEDs) are installed at the reception of every office.

[15.5 Medical Emergencies]
In a medical emergency at the office, call the reception or the security desk immediately; they will contact the nurse, first aiders and an ambulance. Nimbus offices have tie-ups with a nearby hospital for emergency admissions. For emergencies outside the office, call the national emergency number 112. Employees may also call the 24-hour medical helpline provided by our insurance administrator for advice.

[15.6 Workplace Injuries]
Any injury at the workplace, however minor, must be reported to the Safety Officer and recorded in the Facilities section of the IT Service Portal within 24 hours. Injuries that happen during business travel or while commuting in a company-arranged cab are also treated as workplace injuries. The company covers the full cost of treatment for workplace injuries, beyond what is paid by insurance.

[15.7 Ergonomics]
Long hours at a desk can cause back, neck and wrist problems. Adjust your chair so that your feet rest flat on the floor and your screen is at eye level. Use an external keyboard and mouse when working for long periods on a laptop. Take a short break every hour to stand and stretch. Employees with specific ergonomic needs can request an assessment and special equipment through the Facilities section of the IT Service Portal.

[15.8 Women's Safety]
Female employees who leave the office after 8:00 PM are provided with a company cab drop to their home, with a verified driver and GPS tracking. A security guard accompanies the cab if the female employee is the first pickup or the last drop between 10:00 PM and 6:00 AM. The cab app includes an SOS button that alerts the security team and the transport desk immediately.

[15.9 Severe Weather and Natural Disasters]
In case of severe weather such as floods, heavy rain or extreme heat, or natural disasters such as earthquakes, the Crisis Management Team may declare a work from home day for the affected office. Announcements are sent by SMS, email and the NimbusNet app. During an earthquake, drop under a sturdy desk, cover your head and hold on until the shaking stops, then evacuate calmly.

[15.10 Visitors]
All visitors must be registered in advance by their host through the Visitor section of the NimbusNet app. Visitors receive a temporary badge at reception after showing a government-issued photo ID, and must be accompanied by their host at all times. Visitors are not allowed into server rooms or restricted project areas. Hosts are responsible for the conduct and safety of their visitors.

[15.11 Server Rooms and Restricted Areas]
Server rooms, network rooms, the Chennai network operations centre and the Hyderabad data engineering lab are restricted areas. Only employees whose roles require access have their ID cards enabled for these rooms, and every entry is logged. Tailgating, which means following someone through a secure door without tapping your own card, is not allowed anywhere in Nimbus offices. If someone tries to follow you into a restricted area, politely ask them to use their own card or contact security.

[15.12 ID Cards]
Your ID card must be worn visibly at all times inside Nimbus offices and customer sites. If you forget your ID card, collect a temporary one-day card from the security desk after showing photo identification; more than three temporary cards in a month are reported to your manager. Report a lost ID card through the IT Service Portal immediately so that it can be deactivated. A replacement card costs Rs. 300, which is waived for the first replacement.

[15.13 Personal Safety Outside the Office]
When leaving the office late, use well-lit routes and company transport where available. Do not display your laptop or ID card in public places. If you feel unsafe while travelling for work at any time, call the 24-hour security control room on extension 7777 or the number printed on the back of your ID card, and the team will arrange help.

[15.14 Air Quality and Heat]
During periods of very poor air quality in North Indian cities, usually in November, the Mohali and Gurugram offices run air purifiers on every floor, and employees with respiratory conditions may request to work from home. During extreme summer heat, outdoor activities are cancelled and cold drinking water and oral rehydration salts are available at reception. Facilities announces such measures on NimbusNet.

[15.15 Safety Training]
All employees complete a short fire safety and emergency module during induction. Floor Wardens and first aiders receive classroom training once a year, which counts towards their annual learning hours. Volunteers who want to become first aiders can register on the NimbusWell page; at least two first aiders are needed on every floor of every office.

CHAPTER 18: ENGINEERING STANDARDS

[18.1 Why We Have Engineering Standards]
Engineering excellence is one of the CLEAR values. The engineering standards in this chapter describe the minimum practices that every Nimbus project must follow, so that customers receive reliable, secure and maintainable software regardless of which team builds it. Customer contracts may require stricter standards, in which case the stricter rule applies. Exceptions to these standards must be approved by the project's solution architect and recorded on the project's NimbusWiki page.

[18.2 Branching Standard]
All repositories in CodeNest follow trunk-based development. The main branch must always be in a releasable state. Work is done in short-lived feature branches named with the TaskBoard ticket number, for example feature/PAY-1423-add-refund-api. Feature branches should be merged within three working days. Long-running branches, other than release branches for products, need the solution architect's approval.

[18.3 Pull Requests and Code Review]
Every change to the main branch must go through a pull request in CodeNest. Each pull request needs at least one approval from a reviewer other than the author, and changes to security-sensitive code, such as authentication or payment logic, need two approvals including one from a grade L4 or above engineer. Reviewers should respond within one working day. Pull requests should be small, ideally under 400 lines of change, so that they can be reviewed properly.

[18.4 What Reviewers Check]
Reviewers check that the code does what the ticket asks, is readable, follows the project's style guide, has meaningful tests, handles errors properly, does not log sensitive data, and does not introduce security risks. Review comments should be respectful and specific, explaining why a change is suggested. Authors should respond to every comment, either by making the change or by explaining why not.

[18.5 Automated Testing]
Every project must have automated unit tests, and new code must keep the project's unit test coverage at or above 80 percent of lines. Critical business flows must also have automated integration or end-to-end tests. Tests run automatically in LaunchPad on every pull request, and a pull request cannot be merged if any test fails. Flaky tests must be fixed or removed within one sprint; they must never be ignored permanently.

[18.6 Definition of Done]
A user story is considered done only when the code is merged to the main branch, automated tests are written and passing, the code has been reviewed, documentation has been updated where needed, the feature has been deployed to the testing environment, and the product owner has accepted it. Teams may add stricter criteria to their own definition of done but may not remove any of these.

[18.7 Static Analysis and Security Scanning]
LaunchPad runs static code analysis, dependency vulnerability scanning and secret scanning on every pull request. Critical and high-severity vulnerabilities in new code block the merge. Existing vulnerabilities in third-party libraries must be fixed within 15 days for critical issues, 30 days for high, and 90 days for medium. The security team publishes a monthly report of open vulnerabilities for each project.

[18.8 Environments]
Every project has at least three environments: development, testing and production. Larger projects also have a staging environment that mirrors production. Production data must never be copied to development or testing environments unless it is masked. Access to production is limited to named engineers approved by the project's technical lead, and all production access is logged.

[18.9 Release Process]
Production releases are planned in TaskBoard and must have release notes, a rollback plan and approval from the release manager. Standard releases happen on Tuesdays and Thursdays between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM IST, unless the customer requires a different window. Releases on Fridays, weekends and the day before a public holiday need the business unit head's approval, because fewer people are available to fix problems.

[18.10 Year-End Change Freeze]
To protect customers during their busiest period, a change freeze applies to all production systems from 20 December to 5 January every year. During the freeze, only emergency fixes approved by the business unit head and the customer may be deployed. Teams should plan their final releases of the year to finish by 18 December.

[18.11 Incident Severity Levels]
Production incidents are classified into four severity levels. Severity 1 means a complete outage or data loss affecting a customer's business, and the team must respond within 15 minutes. Severity 2 means a major feature is unavailable or badly degraded, with a response within 30 minutes. Severity 3 means a minor feature is affected with a workaround available, with a response within 4 working hours. Severity 4 means a cosmetic issue or question, with a response within 2 working days.

[18.12 Handling a Severity 1 Incident]
When a Severity 1 incident is declared, an incident commander is appointed, an incident bridge is opened on MeetRoom, and the customer is informed within 30 minutes. Status updates are sent to the customer every hour until the service is restored. The business unit head and the Chief Technology Officer are informed automatically through Watchtower. The incident commander decides when the incident is resolved.

[18.13 Post-Incident Reviews]
Every Severity 1 and Severity 2 incident must have a written post-incident review within five working days. Reviews are blameless: they focus on what happened and how to prevent it, not on who made a mistake. The review lists the timeline, root cause, impact, what went well, what could be improved, and action items with owners and dates. Reviews are shared on NimbusWiki and selected reviews are presented at the Thursday Tech Talk.

[18.14 Documentation]
Every project must maintain on NimbusWiki an architecture overview, a setup guide that lets a new engineer run the system locally within one day, runbooks for common operational tasks, and a record of important design decisions. Each repository must have a README file explaining what the code does and how to build and test it. Documentation is reviewed every quarter by the technical lead.

[18.15 Logging Standards]
Applications must write structured logs in JSON format with a timestamp, severity level, service name and correlation ID. Logs must never contain passwords, full card numbers, Aadhaar numbers, authentication tokens or other sensitive personal data. Logs are kept for 30 days in Watchtower by default, or longer if the customer contract requires it.

[18.16 API Design Standards]
Public and internal APIs follow REST conventions with JSON payloads unless a project has a good reason to use another style. APIs are versioned in the URL, for example /api/v1/payments, and must be documented using the OpenAPI specification. Breaking changes require a new version, and old versions must be supported for at least six months after a new version is released.

[18.17 Open Source Use]
Engineers may use open-source libraries in projects, but every library must have a licence on the approved list maintained by the Legal team. Libraries with strong copyleft licences need Legal approval before use in customer projects. Contributing to open-source projects on company time requires approval from your manager, and contributions must not include customer code or confidential information.

[18.18 Architecture Reviews]
Projects with a contract value above Rs. 2 crore, or any project that handles payments, health data or personal data of more than one lakh people, must pass an architecture review before development begins. The review is conducted by the Architecture Review Board, which meets every Wednesday. The project's solution architect presents the design, and the board may approve it, approve it with conditions, or ask for changes.

[18.19 Sprint Rhythm]
Most Nimbus project teams work in two-week sprints. Each sprint begins with sprint planning on Monday morning, includes a 15-minute daily stand-up meeting, and ends with a sprint review with the customer and a team retrospective on the second Friday. Teams may choose a different rhythm, such as one-week sprints or Kanban, if it suits the project better and the customer agrees.

[18.20 Daily Stand-Up]
The daily stand-up is a short meeting, no longer than 15 minutes, where each team member shares what they did yesterday, what they plan to do today, and anything blocking them. Detailed discussions are taken offline after the stand-up with only the people who need to be involved. Distributed teams hold stand-ups on MeetRoom at a time that works across time zones.

[18.21 Retrospectives]
At the end of every sprint, the team holds a retrospective to discuss what went well, what did not go well, and what to try differently in the next sprint. Retrospectives are a safe space; what is said there is not used for individual performance evaluation. Each retrospective should produce no more than three improvement actions with owners, tracked in TaskBoard.

[18.22 Estimation]
Teams estimate user stories using story points, based on relative effort and complexity, rather than hours. Estimates are made by the people who will do the work, not by managers alone. For fixed price projects, estimates in story points are converted into effort ranges by the project manager and solution architect, with a contingency buffer of 15 to 20 percent agreed with the business unit head.

[18.23 Technical Debt]
Technical debt is the extra work created when a quick solution is chosen over a better one. Some technical debt is acceptable when it is a deliberate decision, but it must be recorded in TaskBoard with a Tech Debt label. Teams should spend about 15 percent of every sprint on reducing technical debt, and the technical lead reviews the technical debt list every month.

[18.24 Code Ownership]
Every repository has named owners listed in an owners file at the root of the repository. Owners are automatically added as reviewers to pull requests that change their areas. Shared libraries used by many teams are owned by the Developer Platform team or a community of practice, and changes to them need approval from at least one owner.

CHAPTER 19: DATA PRIVACY

[19.1 Our Privacy Commitment]
Nimbus Softech processes personal data of its employees, its customers' customers, job candidates and business contacts. We are committed to handling all personal data lawfully, fairly and securely, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act and the data protection laws of other countries where our customers operate. The Data Protection Officer, Nandini Krishnan, oversees privacy across the company and can be contacted at privacy@nimbussoftech.example.

[19.2 What Counts as Personal Data]
Personal data is any information that can identify a person, directly or indirectly. Examples include names, phone numbers, email addresses, home addresses, photographs, Aadhaar and PAN numbers, bank details, IP addresses, location data and employee IDs. Some personal data is especially sensitive, such as health information, financial information, biometric data and data about children, and needs extra protection.

[19.3 Privacy Principles]
Every employee who handles personal data must follow six principles. Collect only the data that is needed for a clear purpose. Use the data only for that purpose. Keep the data accurate. Keep it only as long as needed. Protect it with appropriate security. Be transparent with people about how their data is used. When in doubt, ask the Data Protection Officer before using personal data in a new way.

[19.4 Employee Personal Data]
Nimbus Softech collects employee personal data for employment purposes such as payroll, benefits, performance management, security and legal compliance. Employee data is stored in PeopleHub and is accessible only to people who need it for their role, such as HR, payroll and your manager. Employees can view most of their own data in PeopleHub and can request corrections through the HR helpdesk.

[19.5 Customer Personal Data]
When we build or run systems for customers, we usually process personal data on the customer's behalf as a data processor. In that role, we must follow the customer's instructions and the data processing agreement in the contract. Never use customer personal data for any purpose other than delivering the agreed services, such as training AI models or building demos, unless the customer has agreed in writing.

[19.6 Data Retention]
Personal data must not be kept longer than needed. Employee records are kept for eight years after an employee leaves, as required by tax and labour laws. Unsuccessful candidates' data is deleted after one year unless they agree to stay in our talent pool. CCTV footage in offices is kept for 30 days. Customer data is returned or deleted at the end of a project as specified in the contract.

[19.7 Privacy Incidents]
A privacy incident happens when personal data is lost, stolen, accessed without permission or sent to the wrong person. Examples include emailing a salary file to the wrong address, losing a laptop with customer data, or discovering that a database is publicly exposed. Report every privacy incident immediately to security@nimbussoftech.example and privacy@nimbussoftech.example. The company may be legally required to inform regulators and affected people within a short time, so speed matters.

[19.8 Requests From Individuals]
People have rights over their personal data, including the right to access it, correct it and request its deletion. If you receive such a request from an employee, customer, candidate or anyone else, forward it to privacy@nimbussoftech.example within one working day. Do not respond to the request yourself, because the company must verify the person's identity and respond within legal time limits.

[19.9 Privacy by Design]
New projects, products and internal tools that process personal data must consider privacy from the start. The project team completes a privacy impact assessment in NimbusWiki before development begins, describing what data is collected, why, where it is stored, who can access it and how long it is kept. The Data Protection Officer reviews assessments for high-risk processing such as health data or large-scale monitoring.

[19.10 CCTV and Office Security Data]
Nimbus offices use CCTV cameras at entrances, lift lobbies, parking areas and corridors for security purposes. There are no cameras in washrooms, changing rooms, the crèche or prayer rooms. ID card access logs are used for security and attendance only. CCTV footage can be viewed only by the security team, and copies are shared only with HR during an investigation or with police on a lawful request.

CHAPTER 20: WORKING ON CUSTOMER PROJECTS

[20.1 How Projects Are Set Up]
Every customer engagement begins with a signed contract and a statement of work (SOW) stored in LexVault. The SOW describes the scope, deliverables, timelines, team size, pricing model and acceptance criteria. Once the SOW is signed, the Project Management Office creates a project code in FinanceOne, a project in TaskBoard and a space on NimbusWiki. Work must not start without a project code, except with written approval from the business unit head.

[20.2 Pricing Models]
Nimbus Softech uses three main pricing models. In time and materials projects, the customer pays for the actual hours worked at agreed rates, so accurate timesheets are essential. In fixed price projects, the customer pays a fixed amount for an agreed scope, so changes in scope must go through a formal change request. In managed services contracts, the customer pays a monthly fee for running and supporting systems against agreed service levels.

[20.3 Project Roles]
A typical project team includes a project manager or delivery manager, a solution architect, a technical lead, engineers, testers and, where needed, a business analyst, designer and DevOps engineer. The project manager owns the plan, budget, risks and customer communication. The solution architect owns the technical design. The technical lead guides day-to-day engineering decisions and code quality. The account manager owns the overall commercial relationship with the customer.

[20.4 Project Kick-Off]
Every project starts with an internal kick-off and a customer kick-off meeting. The internal kick-off covers the SOW, team roles, risks, the delivery plan and any special customer rules such as background checks, security requirements or working hours. The customer kick-off introduces the team, agrees on communication channels, meeting rhythms, escalation contacts and the definition of done.

[20.5 Status Reporting]
Project managers send a weekly status report to the customer every Friday, covering progress against plan, upcoming work, risks, issues and decisions needed. Internally, projects are rated green, amber or red in TaskBoard every week. A project that is red for two consecutive weeks is reviewed by the business unit head and the Project Management Office, who agree a recovery plan with the project manager.

[20.6 Change Requests]
Any change to the scope, timeline or cost of a project must be documented in a change request, estimated by the team and approved in writing by the customer before the work begins. Engineers should not agree to extra work directly with the customer, even if it seems small; instead, they should inform the project manager, who will handle the change request. Unapproved scope changes are one of the most common reasons fixed price projects lose money.

[20.7 Working at Customer Sites]
Employees deputed to a customer's office must follow both the customer's rules and Nimbus policies. If the two conflict, follow the stricter rule and inform your project manager. Wear your customer badge visibly, do not take photographs inside customer premises, and use only devices approved by the customer on their network. Employees deputed to a customer site for more than three months in another city receive a deputation allowance of Rs. 15,000 per month in addition to accommodation arranged by the company.

[20.8 Customer Escalations]
If a customer raises a complaint or escalation, stay calm and professional, listen carefully, and do not make commitments that you cannot keep. Inform your project manager immediately. The project manager acknowledges the escalation to the customer within four working hours and shares an action plan within two working days. Serious escalations, such as a threat to end the contract, are handled by the account manager and business unit head.

[20.9 Customer Satisfaction Surveys]
Customers are asked to complete a satisfaction survey every six months and at the end of every project. The survey measures quality, communication, responsiveness and value, and includes a Net Promoter Score question. Results are shared with the project team. Projects with a score below 7 out of 10 must hold a review with the business unit head to agree on improvements.

[20.10 Project Closure]
At the end of a project, the project manager obtains formal acceptance from the customer, hands over documentation and access, returns or deletes customer data as required by the contract, releases team members in PeopleHub, closes the project code in FinanceOne and holds a lessons-learned session. Lessons learned are recorded on NimbusWiki so that future projects can benefit from them.

[20.11 Bench and Project Allocation]
Employees who are between projects are said to be on the bench. While on the bench, employees work on internal projects, learning goals, certifications and pre-sales support. The Resource Management team aims to allocate bench employees to a new project within 30 days, considering their skills, career goals and location preferences. Employees can update their skills and preferences in the Skills section of PeopleHub at any time.

CHAPTER 22: COMMUNICATION GUIDELINES

[22.1 Choosing the Right Channel]
Use the right tool for each kind of message. Use NimbusChat for quick questions, coordination and informal discussion within a team. Use email for formal communication, decisions that need a record, messages to customers and anything that will be read later by people outside the conversation. Use MeetRoom calls when a topic needs discussion that would take more than five or six messages back and forth. Use NimbusWiki for information that should last, such as designs, guides and decisions.

[22.2 Email Etiquette]
Write a clear subject line that tells the reader what the email is about and whether action is needed, for example Action needed by Friday: approve March invoices. Keep emails short, put the main point or request in the first two lines, and use bullet points for lists. Use To for people who need to act and Cc for people who only need to know. Avoid Reply All unless everyone on the thread needs your reply. Reply to internal emails within one working day, even if only to say when you will respond fully.

[22.3 Emails to Customers]
Emails to customers represent the company, so write them carefully and professionally. Use the standard Nimbus email signature with your name, designation, office and phone number. Do not share internal discussions, internal pricing or opinions about other customers. For important messages such as delivery dates, escalations or commercial matters, ask your project manager to review the email before sending it.

[22.4 Chat Etiquette]
In NimbusChat, write complete questions instead of just saying hello and waiting for a reply. Use team channels instead of direct messages for work questions, so that others can help and learn from the answer. Use threads to keep conversations organised. Set your status to show when you are in a meeting, on leave or working different hours. Avoid sending work messages to colleagues after 8:00 PM or on weekends unless it is urgent; if you work late, schedule messages to be delivered in the morning.

[22.5 Meeting Guidelines]
Every meeting should have a clear purpose, an agenda shared in advance, and only the people who need to be there. Start and end meetings on time, and prefer 25-minute or 50-minute meetings to leave short gaps between calls. The organiser should share notes with decisions and action items within one working day. Meeting-free time is protected on Friday afternoons from 2:00 PM to 6:00 PM for focused work, except for customer meetings and incidents.

[22.6 Video Call Etiquette]
Join video calls on time, mute yourself when not speaking, and keep your camera on during team meetings and customer calls where possible. Use a neutral or blurred background when working from home. Use headphones to avoid echo. When sharing your screen, close unrelated windows and turn off notifications, especially before customer calls, so that confidential information is not shown by accident.

[22.7 Writing Documents]
Good documents save time for everyone. Start with a short summary that explains the purpose and the main conclusion. Use headings, short paragraphs and diagrams where they help. Write for the reader who knows the least about the topic. Put the date, author and status, such as Draft or Final, at the top of every document. Store documents on NimbusWiki or in the shared project folder, not as attachments in email.

[22.8 Language at Work]
English is the common business language at Nimbus Softech, used for all written communication, documentation and meetings with mixed groups. Employees are welcome to speak any language in informal conversation, but should switch to English when someone in the group does not understand the language being used. Customer communication follows the customer's preferred language where Nimbus has the necessary skills.

[22.9 All-Hands and Town Halls]
The company holds a quarterly all-hands meeting led by the CEO, broadcast from Sky Hall in Mohali and streamed to all offices. Business units hold their own town halls every month. Questions for the leadership team can be submitted in advance, anonymously if preferred, through the Ask Leadership form on NimbusNet. Recordings are available on NimbusLearn for employees who cannot attend live.

CHAPTER 23: HIRING, INTERVIEWS AND INTERNSHIPS

[23.1 How We Hire]
Nimbus Softech hires for skill, attitude and alignment with the CLEAR values. Every role has a written job description and a defined interview process approved by Talent Acquisition. Hiring decisions are made by a panel, not by a single person, to reduce bias. Candidates are assessed on the same criteria for the same role, and all interview feedback is recorded in PeopleHub within 24 hours of the interview.

[23.2 Standard Interview Process]
For most engineering roles, the interview process has four stages: an online coding assessment of 90 minutes, a technical interview of 60 minutes, a system design or problem-solving interview of 60 minutes for grades L3 and above, and a final interview with the hiring manager that includes a values discussion. The full process should be completed within 21 days from the first interview. Candidates receive an update at every stage, even if they are not selected.

[23.3 Becoming an Interviewer]
Employees in grade L2 and above can become interviewers after completing course NL-212 Interviewing Skills and shadowing two interviews with an experienced interviewer. Interviewers are expected to take at least two interviews a month when needed. Each interview completed with feedback on time earns 50 reward points on NimbusNet.

[23.4 Fair Interviewing]
Interviewers must ask only questions related to the job. Never ask candidates about their religion, caste, marital status, plans to have children, age, health or political views. Assess candidates against the written criteria, and write feedback that describes what the candidate did, not general impressions. If you know a candidate personally, declare it and step aside from the panel.

[23.5 Offer and Joining]
Selected candidates receive an offer letter by email through the PeopleHub candidate portal. Offers are valid for seven days. The offer includes the grade, designation, office location, fixed compensation, variable pay or bonus eligibility, and joining date. Candidates may negotiate the joining date with Talent Acquisition within the limits of their current notice period.

[23.6 Campus Hiring]
Every year, Nimbus Softech hires about 250 graduates from engineering colleges across India through campus hiring, which runs from August to December. Campus hires join in grade L1 as Associate Software Engineers, usually between June and August of the following year. They attend a 12-week graduate training programme called Nimbus Launch before joining project teams.

[23.7 Nimbus Launch Programme]
Nimbus Launch is the 12-week training programme for campus hires. The first four weeks cover programming fundamentals, Git, SQL, Linux and agile practices. The next six weeks are a specialisation track in Java and Spring Boot, frontend development, data engineering, quality engineering or cloud and DevOps, based on the graduate's preference and business needs. The final two weeks are a team project built for an internal customer, presented to a panel of senior leaders.

[23.8 Internships]
Nimbus Softech offers internships of two to six months to students in their final or pre-final year of engineering, MCA or MBA programmes. Interns work on real projects under a mentor. The monthly stipend is Rs. 25,000 for engineering and MCA interns and Rs. 30,000 for MBA interns. Interns who perform well may receive a pre-placement offer to join as full-time employees after graduating.

[23.9 Intern Guidelines]
Interns follow the same Code of Conduct, information security rules and office timings as employees. Interns receive a company laptop and an email account for the duration of the internship. Former employees who rejoin Nimbus Softech are known as boomerang employees. CHAPTER 26: OUR PRODUCTS IN DETAIL

[26.1 NimbusPay Overview]
NimbusPay is a cloud-based payments reconciliation platform for banks, payment aggregators, non-banking financial companies and large retailers. It automatically matches transactions from bank statements, payment gateways, UPI switches, card networks and internal ledgers, and highlights mismatches for investigation. NimbusPay processes more than 40 crore transactions a month for over ninety institutions across India and the Middle East.

[26.2 NimbusPay Features]
Key NimbusPay features include rule-based and machine-learning-assisted matching, automatic handling of partial and split payments, exception queues with assignment and ageing reports, chargeback tracking, settlement reports for regulators, and dashboards showing reconciliation rates in real time. NimbusPay connects to banking systems through secure file transfer and APIs, and supports more than 120 statement formats out of the box.

[26.3 NimbusPay Architecture]
NimbusPay is built as a set of Java and Spring Boot microservices running on Kubernetes in a major public cloud, with separate deployments for India and the Middle East to meet data residency rules. Transaction data is stored in a distributed relational database, and matching jobs run on a streaming platform. The product team releases new versions every two weeks, and customers can choose a monthly release channel if they need more time to test.

[26.4 NimbusPay Pricing and Plans]
NimbusPay is sold in three plans. The Starter plan supports up to 10 lakh transactions a month and standard statement formats. The Business plan supports up to 2 crore transactions a month and adds custom matching rules and API access. The Enterprise plan has no transaction limit and adds dedicated support, a private deployment option and a 99.95 percent uptime commitment. Employees should refer customers to the sales team for pricing details.

[26.5 NimbusInsight Overview]
NimbusInsight is a self-service analytics platform that lets business users build dashboards and reports by dragging and dropping fields, without writing SQL. It connects to common databases, cloud data warehouses and spreadsheets. NimbusInsight includes an AI assistant that answers questions in plain English, such as which stores had the highest returns last month, and turns the answer into a chart.

[26.6 NimbusInsight Features]
NimbusInsight features include more than 30 chart types, scheduled email reports, row-level security so that each user sees only the data they are allowed to see, mobile dashboards, alerts when a metric crosses a threshold, and embedding of dashboards inside other applications. The AI assistant always shows the query it ran, so that users can check how an answer was calculated.

[26.7 Product Support]
Product customers are supported by the Product Support team in Bengaluru, which works 24 hours a day for Enterprise customers and from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM IST on working days for other plans. Customers raise tickets through the product support portal. Employees who receive product questions from customers on other projects should forward them to productsupport@nimbussoftech.example rather than answering them directly.

[26.8 Product Roadmap]
The product roadmap for NimbusPay and NimbusInsight is reviewed every quarter by the Chief Technology Officer and the product managers. The roadmap is confidential and must not be shared with customers or partners without approval from the product management team. CHAPTER 29: GUIDE FOR PEOPLE MANAGERS

[29.1 The Role of a Manager at Nimbus]
Managers at Nimbus Softech are responsible for the results of their team and for the growth and wellbeing of the people in it. A good manager sets clear goals, removes obstacles, gives honest feedback, recognises good work, and treats every team member fairly. Managers are also the first point of contact for most HR policies, so they must know this handbook well and ask their HR business partner when unsure.

[29.2 One-to-One Meetings]
Managers must hold a one-to-one meeting with each team member at least once every two weeks, for at least 30 minutes. The one-to-one belongs to the team member: let them set most of the agenda. Use it to discuss progress, blockers, feedback, career goals and wellbeing. Do not cancel one-to-ones repeatedly; if you must, reschedule within the same week.

[29.3 Approving Leave and Attendance]
Managers must act on leave requests within three working days, after which requests escalate automatically. Approve leave unless there is a genuine business reason not to, and if you refuse, suggest alternative dates. Review attendance regularisation requests promptly. Never ask team members to work while on approved leave, except in a genuine emergency, and if you do, offer compensatory time off.

[29.4 Approving Expenses and Timesheets]
Managers approve expense claims and travel requests in PeopleHub and timesheets in TimeTrack. Check that expenses are reasonable, within policy and supported by receipts. Approve timesheets by Monday noon every week, because late approvals delay customer billing. Managers who approve claims that clearly break the travel policy may be asked to explain the approval to Finance.

[29.5 Handling Performance Concerns]
If a team member's performance is below expectations, raise it early and privately, with specific examples and clear expectations. Offer support such as training, pairing or a mentor. Record the discussion in PeopleHub. If performance does not improve after two or three months, speak to your HR business partner about a formal Performance Improvement Plan. Never surprise a team member with a low rating at the end of the year.

[29.6 Supporting Wellbeing]
Watch for signs that a team member is struggling, such as working very long hours, withdrawing from the team or sudden changes in quality. Ask how they are doing and listen without judging. Remind them about the Employee Assistance Programme and their leave options. Respect privacy: do not ask about medical details, and do not share what a team member tells you in confidence, except where safety is at risk.

[29.7 Hiring for Your Team]
To hire a new team member, raise a hiring request in PeopleHub with the job description, grade and budget approval. Work with Talent Acquisition on the interview panel and process. Interview fairly, as described in the Hiring chapter, and make decisions based on the panel's feedback. Prepare for your new joiner's first day by arranging a buddy, access requests and a 30-day onboarding plan.

[29.8 When a Team Member Resigns]
When a team member resigns, speak with them within two working days to understand their reasons. If you want to retain them, involve your HR business partner before making any commitment about salary or role. Agree a handover plan and decide the last working day according to the notice period policy. Treat departing employees respectfully; many former employees return to Nimbus or become customers.

[29.9 Manager Escalation Paths]
For questions about policies, contact your HR business partner. For urgent people issues such as harassment complaints, threats of violence or serious misconduct, contact your HR business partner and the Chief People Officer's office immediately. For security incidents involving your team, contact the security team on extension 7777. For project crises, escalate to your business unit head.

Nimbus Softech wants employees to do great work without burning out. Our wellbeing programme, NimbusWell, covers physical health, mental health, financial wellbeing and social connection. CHAPTER 31: WORKED EXAMPLES OF COMMON POLICIES

[31.1 About These Examples]
The examples in this chapter show how the policies in this handbook apply to common real-life situations. The names are illustrative. When your situation is different, the rules in the relevant chapter apply, and your HR business partner can help.

[31.2 Example: Leave for a mid-year joiner]
Situation: Priya joined Nimbus on 10 March. How much Paid Time Off will she receive in her first year?
How the policy applies: PTO is credited at two days on the first day of each month, and mid-year joiners receive PTO for the remaining full months only. Priya's first full month is April, so she receives PTO for April to December, which is 9 months multiplied by 2 days, or 18 days in her first year.

[31.3 Example: Carrying forward leave]
Situation: On 31 December, Arjun has 16 days of unused Paid Time Off and did not apply for encashment. What happens to his balance?
How the policy applies: Up to 10 days can be carried forward. Arjun carries 10 days into the new year, and the remaining 6 days lapse. If he had applied for encashment between 1 and 15 December, he could have encashed up to 8 days instead of losing them.

[31.4 Example: Sick leave certificate]
Situation: Meera was sick from Monday to Wednesday. Does she need a medical certificate?
How the policy applies: Yes. Sick leave of three or more consecutive days requires a medical certificate uploaded in PeopleHub within three days of returning. For only Monday and Tuesday, no certificate would be needed.

[31.5 Example: Combining casual leave and PTO]
Situation: Rahul wants to take two days of casual leave followed by five days of Paid Time Off for a family trip. Is this allowed?
How the policy applies: No. Casual leave cannot be combined with Paid Time Off in the same continuous absence. Rahul should apply for all seven days as Paid Time Off, or separate the casual leave from the trip.

[31.6 Example: Weekend work for an L2 engineer]
Situation: Sana, a grade L2 engineer, worked a full Saturday at her manager's request to fix a production issue. What does she get?
How the policy applies: Grades L1 to L3 receive one compensatory off for each full day worked on a weekly off at the manager's request. Sana receives one compensatory off, which she must use within 60 days.

[31.7 Example: Weekend work for an L5 lead]
Situation: Vikram, a grade L5 lead, worked on a Sunday during a release. Does he get compensatory off?
How the policy applies: No. Employees in grades L4 and above do not receive compensatory off, but Vikram may agree on flexible time off with his manager. If he was on the on-call roster, he would receive the on-call allowance instead.

Situation: Kiran was on call for one week and handled two incidents that each took more than one hour outside office hours. How much on-call allowance does he receive?
How the policy applies: The on-call allowance is Rs. 3,000 per week plus Rs. 1,000 for each incident requiring more than one hour outside office hours. Kiran receives Rs. 3,000 plus Rs. 2,000, which is Rs. 5,000 in total.

Situation: Neha worked the US shift for 18 nights in a month. What shift allowance does she receive?

How the policy applies: The per diem is reduced by 25 percent for each meal provided. For grades L1 and L2 in a Category A city, the per diem is Rs. 1,200, so with breakfast included Deepak receives Rs. 900 per day.

Situation: Harsh, on a business trip to Lucknow, stays with his uncle instead of a hotel. What can he claim?

CHAPTER 34: GLOSSARY

[34.1 About the Glossary]
This glossary explains common terms, abbreviations and system names used at Nimbus Softech and in this handbook.

[34.2 Anchor days]
Anchor days: the days of the week when a team agrees to be in the office together, usually Tuesday and Wednesday.

[34.3 Architecture Review Board]
Architecture Review Board: the group of senior architects that reviews designs of large or high-risk projects every Wednesday.

[34.4 Bench]
Bench: the state of being between project allocations, when employees work on internal projects and learning.

[34.5 Business unit]
Business unit: one of the three customer-facing divisions: Financial Services, Retail and Logistics, or Healthcare and Education.

[34.6 Calibration]
Calibration: meetings where managers review ratings together to keep them fair across teams.

[34.7 Category A city]
Category A city: a city with the highest travel limits, such as Mumbai, Delhi NCR or Bengaluru.

[34.8 Change freeze]
Change freeze: the period from 20 December to 5 January when production changes are not allowed except emergencies.

[34.9 Change request]
Change request: a formal document that records and approves any change to a project's scope, timeline or cost.

[34.10 CLEAR]
CLEAR: the five core values: Customer first, Learning always, Engineering excellence, Accountability and Respect.

[34.11 CodeNest]
CodeNest: the company's source code platform for Git repositories and pull requests.

[34.12 Compensatory off]
Compensatory off: a day off given to grades L1 to L3 for working on a weekly off or holiday, valid for 60 days.

[34.13 Core hours]
Core hours: 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM, when every employee must be available for collaboration.

[34.14 CTC]
CTC: cost to company, the total annual cost of an employee including salary, Provident Fund and gratuity.

[34.15 Definition of done]
Definition of done: the list of conditions a user story must meet before it is considered complete.

[34.16 Deputation]
Deputation: a temporary assignment to work at a customer's site.

[34.17 EAP]
EAP: the Employee Assistance Programme, which offers free confidential counselling.

[34.18 Floor Warden]
Floor Warden: a trained volunteer who guides people during evacuations.

[34.19 Full and final settlement]
Full and final settlement: the final payment made to an employee who leaves, processed within 45 days.

[34.20 Gratuity]
Gratuity: a payment for employees who complete five years of service, calculated at 15 days of basic salary per year.

[34.21 Hot desk]
Hot desk: a desk that is not permanently assigned and is booked through the NimbusNet app.

[34.22 HR business partner]
HR business partner: the HR person assigned to support employees and managers of a business unit.

[34.23 IC]
IC: the Internal Committee that handles complaints of sexual harassment.

[34.24 IJP]
IJP: Internal Job Posting, the board in PeopleHub where open roles are shown to employees first.

[34.25 KeyKeeper]
KeyKeeper: the secrets manager used to store passwords, API keys and certificates for applications.

[34.26 LaunchPad]
LaunchPad: the build and deployment pipeline system.

[34.27 LOP]
LOP: Loss of Pay, an unpaid absence when no leave balance is available.

[34.28 MFA]
MFA: multi-factor authentication, which requires an authenticator app in addition to your password.

[34.29 NimbusConnect]
NimbusConnect: the company VPN for working outside the office.

[34.30 NimbusHack]
NimbusHack: the company-wide hackathon held in July and January.

[34.31 NimbusLearn]
NimbusLearn: the learning platform that hosts courses and tracks learning hours.

[34.32 NimbusNet]
NimbusNet: the company intranet and mobile app.

[34.33 NimbusVault]
NimbusVault: the company-approved password manager.

[34.34 NOC]
NOC: the network operations centre in Chennai that monitors production systems 24x7.

[34.35 PeopleHub]
PeopleHub: the HR platform for leave, payroll, expenses, travel, performance and exits.

[34.36 Per diem]
Per diem: a fixed daily allowance for meals and incidental expenses during business travel.

[34.37 PIP]
PIP: a Performance Improvement Plan of 60 to 90 days for employees who need to improve performance.

[34.38 PMO]
PMO: the Project Management Office, which sets up projects and monitors project health.

[34.39 Post-incident review]
Post-incident review: a blameless written analysis of a Severity 1 or 2 incident, due within five working days.

[34.40 PTO]
PTO: Paid Time Off, also called earned leave, 24 days per year.

[34.41 Restricted holiday]
Restricted holiday: an optional festival holiday; every employee may choose 2 per year.

[34.42 Sabbatical]
Sabbatical: unpaid leave of one to six months available after five years of service.

[34.43 Severity 1]
Severity 1: the highest incident level, meaning a complete outage or data loss, requiring a 15-minute response.

[34.44 SOW]
SOW: statement of work, the document that defines a project's scope, deliverables and pricing.

[34.45 TaskBoard]
TaskBoard: the project tracking tool for stories, bugs and sprints.

[34.46 TimeTrack]
TimeTrack: the timesheet system, where timesheets are due every Friday by 6:00 PM.

[34.47 Top-up insurance]
Top-up insurance: optional extra health cover that applies after the base cover of Rs. 5,00,000 is used up.

[34.48 Watchtower]
Watchtower: the monitoring and alerting platform for logs, metrics and traces.

[34.49 Wellness Day]
Wellness Day: a paid day off for rest, two per year, which needs no reason.

[34.50 NimbusWell]
NimbusWell: the employee wellbeing programme covering physical, mental, financial and social wellbeing.

[34.51 Right to disconnect]
Right to disconnect: the principle that employees need not respond to work messages outside working hours unless on call.

[34.52 Nimbus Launch]
Nimbus Launch: the 12-week training programme for campus graduates.

[34.53 Returnship]
Returnship: a six-month paid programme for professionals returning after a career break.

[34.54 Boomerang employee]
Boomerang employee: a former employee who rejoins Nimbus Softech.

[34.55 Story points]
Story points: a relative measure of effort and complexity used to estimate user stories.

[34.56 Technical debt]
Technical debt: extra future work created by choosing a quick solution over a better one.

[34.57 Deputation allowance]
Deputation allowance: Rs. 15,000 per month paid for long assignments at a customer site in another city.

[34.58 NimbusCares]
NimbusCares: the corporate social responsibility programme.

[34.59 Hot Job]
Hot Job: a hard-to-fill role that earns an extra Rs. 15,000 referral bonus.

[34.60 Secure release printing]
Secure release printing: printing where a job is held until you tap your ID card at the printer.
